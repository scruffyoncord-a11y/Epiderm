How it works

A careful check, in four clear steps.

  1. 01 / CHOOSE

    Choose a category

    Paste the message or select the file that concerns you.

  2. 02 / ISOLATE

    A private container opens

    A throwaway, locked-down space is created just for your check.

  3. 03 / COMPARE

    Evidence is checked

    The strongest signals are compared. Unknowns stay unknown.

    You get a next step

    See the reasoning, limits, and a checklist. You make the decision.

Private by design

Untrusted files stay inside a throwaway room.

Files and email headers are read in a private container with no network, no privileges, a read-only disk, and limited memory. It disappears when you leave.

If the sandbox cannot safely open a file, Epiderm refuses it. The file is never opened somewhere less safe instead.

Local first. The reasoning model runs on your computer by default. If you choose Gemini or Claude instead, Epiderm tells you before text is sent to a third party.

Honest about limits

Evidence helps.
It does not prove.

Metadata can be changed

A clean image or document history is useful context, not proof of origin.

Email checks have boundaries

Passing SPF, DKIM, or DMARC does not prove that the message itself is honest.

No invented official addresses

If an organisation is not in the curated list, Epiderm says “no verified record.”

Hostile text is expected

Content that tries to command the checker is treated as a warning sign, not an instruction.

FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

Does Epiderm decide whether something is a scam?+

No. It gives one of three suggested actions—allow, extra check, or verify independently—and explains the evidence. The final decision stays with you.

Does my message leave my computer?+

Not by default. Epiderm uses Gemma through Ollama on your computer. You can choose Gemini or Claude, but the app clearly tells you when that sends text to a third party.

Can it check links or attachments?+

Link and URL analysis and attachment scanning are not available yet. Epiderm can inspect supported documents you upload directly.

Can it detect deepfake video or cloned voices?+

Not yet. Image metadata checks are partly available. Voice cloning, video deepfake detection, and face matching are planned for later.
